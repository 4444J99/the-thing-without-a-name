The film is a pure function of a seed and a time — f(seed, t) — with no accumulated state anywhere, so any frame can be computed without computing the one before it. It renders in WebGL2 through a single projector matrix reconstructed from the 2017 camera; every fragment samples its pixel through that one matrix, which is why planes at unrelated depths and angles still meet along the same floor line.

The 2017 composite is not re-created by eye. It was solved back into two hundred fifty-six rectangles, each naming the photograph it came from, and the engine reproduces the original at 31.60 dB PSNR against an arithmetic ceiling of 31.61 dB — the remaining error is image compression, not the model.

Delivered at 3840×2160, 30 fps, ProRes 422 HQ, from full-camera-resolution plates. Sound is stereo at −16 LUFS integrated with true peak below −1 dBTP, built by granular selection from 193 seconds of the same apartment: no oscillator, no artificial reverberation. The whole film, picture and sound, is reproducible from one 32-bit number, which is printed on the last frame.
