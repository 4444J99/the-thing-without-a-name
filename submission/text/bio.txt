Anthony J. Padavano is a photographer and programmer working between South Florida and New York. He teaches at Broward College. His work is built on the idea that a picture is a record of a place a camera stood, and that everything else — depth, motion, a body — can be recovered from enough of those records. He writes the software his images are made with, and treats the program and the photograph as one object rather than two. THE THING WITHOUT A NAME is his first generative film, made from a photographic study begun in 2017.
